About

Data science that ships to production.

I'm a Senior Data Scientist at Shift Technology, where I work on the ML and data systems that power fraud detection for insurers around the world. My work sits where modelling meets engineering: feature pipelines, entity resolution, and the data layers that keep models fast and reliable for every client.

I hold an MSc in Data Science from HEC Montréal and have published ML research in the Journal of Economic Surveys, using topic models to map thirty years of finance literature. Before data science, I was a backend software engineer, which is why I care as much about latency and reliability as I do about model quality.

Outside work, you'll find me on long hikes, reading, or over a warm cup of tea.
